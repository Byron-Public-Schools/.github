# Are you a student of Byron Public Schools?
You can become a member of this organization if you are!

## 👋 Welcome!

Welcome to the unofficial code repository home for student developers, tech enthusiasts, and creators in the Byron Public Schools community! Whether we're building tools for class projects, automating workflows, exploring robotics, or creating open-source utilities, this space is dedicated to student-driven innovation.

Our district mission is to **Learn! Share! Innovate! Inspire!** — and we aim to live by that through code.

---

## 🚀 What You'll Find Here

Because this organization is managed by students, projects here vary widely based on what we are learning and building. You might stumble across:
* **Student Tools:** Utility apps, homework trackers, or study aids created to make school life easier.
* **Club Projects:** Repositories from student-led clubs (like Robotics, Coding Club, or eSports).
* **Class Experiments:** Collaborative coding projects, web development practice, and computer science portfolios.
* **Open Source Playgrounds:** Places where we test out new frameworks, languages, and ideas.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Interests

Our student developers love experimenting with a variety of languages and tools:
* **Languages:** Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML/CSS, Java, C++
* **Tools & Frameworks:** Git, React, Node.js, Arduino/Raspberry Pi (for hardware/robotics)

---

## 🤝 Get Involved (For Students!)

Are you a student at Byron High School, Middle School, or intermediate/primary who loves coding, building, or designing? You're welcome to join or collaborate! 

1. **Got a project?** Open an issue or submit a pull request if you want to share your work.
2. **Want to collaborate?** Reach out to fellow student contributors to team up on local tech projects.
3. **Keep it clean:** Please ensure any code or assets shared here follow school conduct guidelines and respect copyright/licenses.
